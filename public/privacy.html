Privacy Policy

What Spliinter stores

Spliinter is a real-time word game. During a game, the server temporarily stores room codes, usernames, connection details, submitted words, scores, and game state in memory so the game can work. This data is normally removed when the room closes or the server restarts.

Browser storage

Spliinter uses session storage to remember your username and reconnect you to a room in the same browser tab. The consent choice is stored in local storage. These are not advertising cookies. You can clear them through your browser settings.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is configured for page-view and game-activity measurement. Analytics storage is denied by default and is enabled only after you accept optional cookies. Custom game events record actions such as creating or joining a room, starting a game, requesting a rematch, and submitting a word; the username, room code and word text are not included in those event parameters. Google may process technical information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy. You can reject or change your optional cookie choice using Cookie settings. Advertising is not currently served by the game.

Service providers

Spliinter may be hosted by a cloud provider and may use infrastructure providers to deliver the website and real-time connections. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address and connection logs to operate and secure the service.

Your choices and rights

You may stop using Spliinter and clear browser storage at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of personal information. Contact us to make a request.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact contact@spliinter.in.
