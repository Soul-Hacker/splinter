Play a free multiplayer word game online

Find words hidden in a shared long word, challenge friends in real time, and score points for words only you find. Play in your browser with no download or account required.

  • Create a room and share its code or link, or join someone else's. The host picks how many rounds to play, from 1 to 10. Every round, everyone in the room gets the same word and 2 minutes on the clock.
  • Each word needs 3 or more letters, made only from the letters in the base word, never using a letter more often than the base word has it.
  • Words must be in the dictionary. The base word itself doesn't count.
  • When time runs out, a word that two or more players found is crossed out for everyone. Each word only you found earns 1 point.
  • The best score in a round wins gold, then silver and bronze. After the last round, the highest total wins the game.

How a round works

One word for everyone. Two minutes. Only unique finds count.

Everyone in a room gets the same base word and the same server-timed round. Find smaller words using its letters; a letter can appear in your answer only as many times as it appears in the base word. The tiles dim as you use letters, giving you a visual check before you submit.

When the timer ends, everyone’s word lists are revealed together. A word submitted by two or more players is crossed out and earns no points. Each word submitted by one player earns that player one point, regardless of its length.

Worked example

What a round of CHOCOLATE could look like

CHOCOLATE has nine tiles: two Cs, two Os, and one each of H, L, A, T and E. Here is a sample reveal for three players.

AshaCOAT TACO EACH HOTEL LOCATE2
BenCOAT EACH COOL CHEAT TEACH2
ChloeTACO COOL ECHO CHALET COACH3

Green means one player found it: 1 point. Crossed out means two or more found it: 0 points for everyone.

COAT, TACO, EACH and COOL were shared, so they scored nothing. COACH is legal because CHOCOLATE contains two Cs. LOCAL would be rejected: it needs two Ls, but CHOCOLATE has only one. Chloe wins the round with three points; Asha and Ben tie with two and share silver. Tied medals skip the next rank, so there is no bronze in this example.

Why it plays differently

Length doesn’t matter. Being the only one who saw it does.

Many word games reward long answers. Spliinter instead gives one point to each word only one player found, so a short, surprising answer can be worth more than a long word everyone spotted.

  • Start with easy words, then move on. Common finds can still be worth submitting, but expect duplicates in a larger room.
  • Search for word families. Once you have a stem, test nearby endings and beginnings—but check each form against the available letters and dictionary.
  • Count letters, not just letter types. LOCAL uses letters that appear in CHOCOLATE, but asks for two Ls where only one is available.

Rooms

Play together without accounts or downloads

Private by link

Create a room and share its code or invite link with friends. Private rooms do not appear in the public room list.

Public room list

Choose whether to list your room publicly. A room accepts players until it reaches its chosen limit or the game starts.

Host controls

The host chooses a room size of 2–12 players and a game length of 1–10 rounds. If the host disconnects, the longest-present connected player takes over.

Reconnect to a game

During a game, refresh in the same browser tab to try to resume with your words and score. Reconnection depends on the room still existing in the running server process.

Fair by design

The server checks every submission

The server enforces the round deadline, checks letter counts and dictionary membership, and scores duplicates after the round. Other players’ submitted words stay hidden until the reveal. The dictionary is loaded by the server from the word-list package, with a small set of additional words; see how the game is built for more detail.

Quick answers

Before your first game

Do I need an account?

No. Choose a username to identify yourself in a room. It is not a permanent account, and the game does not ask you to install an app.

Can I play on my own?

A room needs at least two players to start. Join a public room if one is available, or create one and share its link with someone.

Why was my word rejected?

It may be too short, use a letter too many times, match the base word, or be missing from the dictionary. Read why letter counts matter or visit the FAQ.

What happens if I refresh?

The same browser tab tries to reconnect using temporary session information. It can resume only while the original room remains in the running server’s memory; a server restart or closed room cannot be recovered.
